Shorts Uploader

Shorts Uploader helps video creators post their finished short videos to TikTok. You sign in with TikTok, pick a video, review the caption, choose who can see it and which interactions to allow, and post it to your own account. You can also send the video to your TikTok inbox as a draft and finish it in the TikTok app.

Shorts Uploader only posts when you press Post, and only to the TikTok account you signed in with. Videos made with AI are labelled as AI-generated content on TikTok.
