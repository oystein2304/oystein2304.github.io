Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Shorts Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data we use

When the owner connects a TikTok or YouTube account, the tool receives an access token and basic account information (such as the display name) from that platform. These are used only to upload and publish the owner's videos.

What we do not do

We do not collect data about viewers or other users, we do not sell or share any data, and we do not use data for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private cloud environment. The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok or Google account settings, which makes the stored tokens useless.
