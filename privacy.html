Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Shorts Uploader is a tool that lets a creator post their own short videos to their own TikTok account.

What data we use

When you sign in with TikTok, we receive an access token and basic account information (display name, username and avatar). We show your name and avatar on the posting screen so you can confirm the account, and we use the token only to upload and post the videos you choose.

What we do not do

We do not collect data about viewers or other users, we do not sell or share any data, and we do not use data for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Your videos are uploaded straight to TikTok and are not stored by us. Access tokens are kept only for the posting session, or as encrypted secrets when you set up automatic drafts. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings under Security and permissions, which makes any stored token useless.
